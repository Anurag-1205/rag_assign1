# Corpus: dpdp_corpus.pdf

A single 39-page PDF built from two Government of India Gazette publications, so both retrieval pipelines run over one document.

| Pages in `dpdp_corpus.pdf` | Document | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1-21 | The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023), Gazette of India Extraordinary, 11 Aug 2023 | https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-09/DPDT_11082024.pdf (copy of the Gazette text; the Act is also on India Code) |
| 22-39 | The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E)), Gazette of India Extraordinary, 13 Nov 2025 | https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf (pages 24-41 of the original) |

Notes:
- The original Rules PDF is bilingual. Its Hindi pages (1-23) use a legacy font that extracts as unreadable text, so only the English pages were kept.
- The pages were merged unchanged; nothing in the text was edited.
- The corrigendum to the Rules (G.S.R. 892(E), Dec 2025) only fixes typographical errors and is not included.
